● All looks correct. Here's what changed and how it works:

  - App.js — added handleCollectionUpdated(collectionId) which reads the
  collections array from localStorage, stamps the matching collection's
  dateUpdated with the current time, and writes it back. Passed to ItemsPage as
  onCollectionUpdated.
  - ItemsPage.js — calls onCollectionUpdated?.(collection.id) in three places:
    - After saving an item (add or edit)
    - After deleting an item
  - Why it works on navigate-back — CollectionsPage loads its collections from
  localStorage on mount. Since the timestamp is written to localStorage before
  the user navigates back, it picks up the fresh dateUpdated and sorts that
  collection to the top of the unpinned list automatically.
