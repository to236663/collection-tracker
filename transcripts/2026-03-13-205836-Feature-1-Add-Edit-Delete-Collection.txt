with the collection below it. Make sure that the hovering of the dropdown menus 
 does not interfere with the hovering of the collection cards.

  later in the DOM naturally paint above earlier ones — so the dropdown from
  card A gets buried under card B, causing visual bleed-through on hover. The
  fix is to elevate the card whose menu is open above its siblings.
